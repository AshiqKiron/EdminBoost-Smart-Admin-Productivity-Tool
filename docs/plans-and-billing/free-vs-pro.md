# Free vs Pro

This page compares the **Free**, **Pro**, and **Agency** plans shown on the in-plugin Billing page.

## Important: WordPress.org build

If you installed EdminBoost from the **WordPress.org plugin directory**, you already have the **full feature set**. Pro and Agency are **optional commercial licenses** for site licensing and direct vendor support — not locked functionality inside the free plugin.

---

## Quick comparison

| | **Free** | **Pro** | **Agency** |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Annual price** | $0 | $49 | $99 |
| **Site license** | Unlimited | 1 site | 10 sites |
| **Best for** | Full plugin on any site | One licensed production site | Up to 10 client sites |

## When to purchase Pro or Agency

Purchase a license when you want:

- A **licensed Pro or Agency package** from Asphalt Themes
- **Priority email support** from the plugin author
- A **1-site or 10-site license pack** for commercial deployments

You do **not** need to upgrade to unlock drawer panels, white label, export/import, premium themes, or any other tool in the WordPress.org build.

For prices and checkout, open **EdminBoost → Billing** in wp-admin or see [How to upgrade](upgrading.md).
